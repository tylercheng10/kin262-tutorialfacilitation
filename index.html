Optimal Benchmark Allocation ($500B / 10 Years)

  • 1. Post-ER Care & LTC (ALC Liberation): $175B (35%)
  • 2. Complex Care & Outpatient Teams: $125B (25%)
  • 3. Doctor Shortage & Primary Care: $125B (25%)
  • 4. Hospital Space (Acute Care Infrastructure): $75B (15%)

Why This Strategy Yields the Highest Score:

  1. Unlocking the "Bed-Blocking" Bottleneck ($175B - 35%): Up to 20–25% of hospital beds are held by ALC patients who are ready to leave but have no post-acute destination. Investing heavily here frees up existing acute beds far cheaper than building new hospital wings.
  2. Upstream Deflection ($125B - 25%): Medically complex patients account for disproportionate ER visits. Preventive outpatient care suppresses ER inflow before patients hit crisis levels.
  3. Compounding Primary Care ($125B - 25%): Training doctors takes time (3–5 year lag). Funding GP capacity yields compounding rewards over the 10-year timeline.
  4. Avoiding the "Acute-Care Trap" ($75B - 15%): Expanding hospital space without fixing post-acute care simply creates expensive beds that get immediately blocked by ALC patients.
